Compliance Status.

What compliance means for a learner in a perpetual RQI curriculum, and how each status is determined.

01Definition

A learner is compliant when every activity due in their curriculum has been completed, and completed on or before the date it was due.

Compliance is stated at two grains, from the same facts. A live status describes one learner as of now and answers whether action is needed. A session record describes one session that was due and answers whether it was completed, and whether on time. Both are produced by the same five-stage determination in section 04.

A session is complete only in full. Completing some of its activities but not all does not constitute completion. Where a curriculum carries prerequisites, those prerequisites must also be complete.

For a session to count for a learner at all, the session must have been due with a due date, and the learner must have been active at their organisation on that due date.

Applies to

  • Learners enrolled in a perpetual curriculum, meaning one with recurring sessions that carry due dates
  • Learners who have reached their first perpetual session
  • Sessions that were due, with a due date, while the learner was active at the organisation

Does not apply to

  • Once-and-done curricula such as Entry and Prep. These carry no due date, so they produce an eCard status only
  • Learners before their first perpetual session becomes available
  • Sessions that fell due when the learner was not active at the organisation
  • eCard validity. An eCard status describes whether an eCard is valid, expired, or not applicable for a program. The two often agree, which is why they are confused, but they are different concepts

Written for a quarterly curriculum. The structure holds for other cadences by substituting the cadence wherever period appears.

03Status model

Every determination resolves into one of three outcome families. Keeping them apart is what stops a broken record being read as a clean result. This three-family model is intended as a reusable pattern for other business definitions in the domain.

Status determined

The definition applies and a status was produced: one of four live statuses, or one of three session outcomes.

Consumer responseAct on the status.

Not applicable

The definition correctly does not apply. Not enrolled, a once-and-done curriculum, before the first perpetual session, or a session that fell due while the learner was not active. This is the absence of a status, not a failing one.

Consumer responseNone. Investigate only if the exclusion itself looks wrong, which is how administrative errors surface.

Cannot determine

The definition applies but required information is missing or contradictory. Distinct from Not applicable, and deliberately so: a defect must not be recorded as a legitimate exclusion, because nobody audits legitimate exclusions.

Consumer responseCorrect the record. Do not act on the learner.

The two status sets

Live status

One per learner, as of now. Consumed by Unit and Program Administrators.

Compliant
The learner is compliant and the current session is complete.
Session incomplete
The learner is compliant but still needs to complete the current session.
Dependency incomplete
The curriculum is complete but not all dependencies needed to extend eCard validity are. ALS and PALS only.
Overdue
Activities from prior periods are past due, so the learner is not compliant.

Session record

One per session that was due, retained. Consumed by analysis and reporting.

Completed on-time
All activities completed by their due date that period.
Completed after due date
All activities complete, but at least one after the due date, so the session was not on time.
Not yet completed
The period's assignment is not complete. Once it is, it becomes one of the two above.

04How a status is determined

Compliance is determined through five business decision stages, evaluated in order. Each stage must resolve before the next is meaningful.
